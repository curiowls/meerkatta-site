## Editorial rules

- Read `docs/field-notes/template.md` before every draft. Preserve founder reflection, selective analogies, and current-versus-future boundaries; add scannable headings, short lead phrases, and purposeful visual/evidence modules. Do not turn notes into attribute lists.

- For future notes, seek credible references where they genuinely support the topic or a passage: primary research or original writing by a respected, relevant expert. Read and link the source; distinguish findings, opinion, and our interpretation. No forced citations or implied product validation. See the template reference guideline.
- Aim for 300-500 words excluding references and one useful observation, practice, or product decision. No daily filler for SEO.
- Review existing journal pages and previous drafts before writing. Revisit a subject only with a concrete new situation or question.
- Use the founder's calm, direct, curious voice. Do not invent personal anecdotes, customer quotes, usage results, or performance evidence.
- MeerKatta cleans and structures what people say; it does not execute instructions or invent their reply. Label illustrative outputs; measured comparisons need real tests.
- Verify current app behavior before describing settings, platform parity, offline support, key providers, privacy, pricing, or shortcuts. Ask here when unclear.
- Reuse approved artwork or real product visuals. Propose new graphics before generating. Include helpful alt text and stable responsive dimensions.
- Keep English first for these journal drafts; do not imply untranslated notes are localized.
- Record draft path and status below. Drafted is not published. Preserve owner edits and unrelated repository work.
