Productivity entry point

Use voice when typing slows the thought down.

Dictation is faster than typing for many notes, prompts, messages, and draft paragraphs. MeerKatta supports that practical need, then adds a layer for the messy part: false starts, filler, rough phrasing, and thoughts that change direction while you speak.

MeerKatta difference

The goal is not only speed.

The deeper problem is that typing can turn you into an editor before the thought exists. MeerKatta helps you keep speaking until the idea has a body, then gives you text you can keep, share, or build from.
