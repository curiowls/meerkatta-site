Dictation keyboard for iPhone & iPad

Speak into the app you already have open.

The MeerKatta keyboard runs inside standard text fields on both iPhone and iPad—giving you a clean record-and-stop surface, quick punctuation keys after insertion, and contextual Return or Send buttons.

How the MeerKatta keyboard works on iPhone and iPad

Once enabled in iOS or iPadOS Settings, the MeerKatta keyboard sits alongside your standard Apple keyboard so you can dictate directly into messages, emails, reminders, and notes.

  • Allow Full Access: iOS requires Full Access for third-party keyboards that handle voice dictation, read your voice profile and custom vocabulary settings, and send speech to your chosen processing service.
  • Speak, stop, and insert: tap the microphone on the MeerKatta keyboard, speak your rough draft—including pauses and mid-sentence corrections—then tap stop to transcribe, polish, and insert the cleaned text at your cursor.
  • Quick controls after insertion: once MeerKatta inserts cleaned text at your cursor, quick punctuation keys (@ # . , ? ! '), numbers/symbols/emoji modes, and a contextual Return or Send button appear so you can finish the message without switching back right away.

Honest limits to know up front: Custom keyboards cannot appear in secure password fields or apps that disable third-party keyboards. MeerKatta transcribes and polishes after you tap Stop rather than streaming words live as you speak. Review names, dates, and numbers before sending.

iPhone Lock Screen & Live Activity

Put quick capture where your thumb can reach it.

A Lock Screen shortcut only saves a passing thought after you place it where you will see it. MeerKatta gives you two distinct entry points—a parked Quick Capture widget and an active Live Activity banner.

→

Quick Capture widget vs. Live Activity

Quick Capture Widget (always parked where you put it): One MeerKatta widget supports three slots—Lock Screen circular, Lock Screen rectangular, and Home Screen small (2×2). Tapping it opens MeerKatta directly into the capture drawer so you can speak immediately, tap stop, and review the cleaned note saved in your Library.

Live Activity & Dynamic Island (while a session is active): During an active session, MeerKatta displays a Lock Screen banner (Keep your flow · Continue thought) and Dynamic Island status on supported iPhones so you can see progress or pick up a thought without hunting for the app icon.

Clear boundary on unlock & commands: The widget opens MeerKatta's capture screen—it is not a promise to bypass Face ID, Touch ID, passcode unlock, or OS permission prompts. And capture saves your words as a note; saying “ask Sam about the projector” keeps the reminder for you rather than sending Sam a message automatically.

01

Customize your Lock Screen

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose your Lock Screen, and authenticate if your iPhone asks.

02

Choose the Quick Capture widget

Tap the widget row (or Add Widgets), choose MeerKatta, and add the circular or rectangular Quick Capture slot. Remove an existing widget if the row is full.

03

Tap Done and test once

Close the widget picker, tap Done, and run one 10-second test capture while comfortably stopped so you know how the drawer feels before you need it on a walk.

Setup reference: Apple Support: How to add and edit widgets on your iPhone. Labels and placement can vary slightly by iOS and iPadOS version.

FAQ

iPhone & iPad voice typing details.

Why does the MeerKatta keyboard ask for Allow Full Access?

iOS and iPadOS restrict third-party keyboards by default unless Allow Full Access is turned on. MeerKatta needs Allow Full Access so the keyboard can record voice dictation, read your voice profile and custom vocabulary settings, and send audio to your chosen transcription and polishing service. MeerKatta only records after you explicitly tap the microphone button.

Does the Lock Screen widget bypass Face ID or start listening on its own?

No. Adding the Quick Capture widget puts an entry point on your Lock Screen or Home Screen that opens MeerKatta directly into the capture drawer when you tap it. It does not promise bypassing Face ID, Touch ID, passcode unlock, or OS microphone permissions, and MeerKatta is never always-on listening.

Does the MeerKatta keyboard work in every iPhone and iPad app?

It works in standard text fields across most apps, including Messages, Mail, Reminders, Notes, and chat apps. Like any iOS or iPadOS third-party keyboard, it cannot appear in secure password fields or apps that disable custom keyboards. When an app does not allow custom keyboards, you can capture inside MeerKatta and copy or share the cleaned note.

What happens if I lose signal or want backup transcription on iPhone or iPad?

Apple Speech is available as an optional backup transcription engine in Providers & Keys on supported devices and languages; cloud transcription does not require Speech Recognition permission. Recovery audio on iPhone and iPad is kept locally for up to seven days rather than as a permanent audio archive.

How is MeerKatta priced on iPhone and iPad, and do I need an account?

Get MeerKatta for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch on the App Store. Apple displays local prices, managed subscription or one-time MeerKatta + Your AI (BYOK) options, and trial eligibility before you buy. No MeerKatta account is required to buy or restore App Store purchases, and optional sign-in links an eligible purchase across Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.
