FAQ

Practical details.

Which devices can I use MeerKatta on?

MeerKatta is available for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Download the Mac app, or get MeerKatta for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch on the App Store. Record and listen back on your Watch; recordings are processed when they reach your iPhone.

Is MeerKatta just dictation?

MeerKatta is a voice dictation and thought-capture app for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Speak into a working text field, or save a thought before you know where it belongs. It turns rough speech into clearer, structured text while keeping your intent.

Why not just use free dictation?

Free dictation is useful when you already know exactly what to say. MeerKatta is for the earlier, more fragile moment when you are forming the thought and do not want editing to interrupt it.

Does MeerKatta rewrite my voice?

The goal is polish, not replacement. MeerKatta should make the thought clearer while keeping it recognizably yours.

Where do audio and video fit?

Audio and video refinement are in the making and are not available in the current release. We’re exploring how AI-guided processing could remove filler, clarify the structure, and return a cleaner recording in the same medium—audio as audio, video as video. Scope and release timing are not settled.
